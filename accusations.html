✅ Case Closed — Solved

Sienna Clarke

Sienna Clarke doesn't flinch — until you place the locker photograph and the solved cryptogram on the table in front of her.

Then she does.

"...Then you already know everything that matters."

The confession takes forty minutes. The trail was deliberate — every theft a breadcrumb toward the vault beneath the New Theatre Royal. Arthur Vale's life work. Arthur Vale's obsession. Arthur Vale, who is now nowhere to be found.

The vault is opened. Six pieces of Captain Hale's collection, intact. A logbook — the captain's own, barely legible after four centuries — listing more. Much more.

Sienna Clarke is remanded in custody. Arthur Vale remains missing. The logbook, they say, is the beginning of a longer story.
